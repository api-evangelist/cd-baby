CD Baby President Molly Neuman shared key insights at The Great Escape music festival, emphasizing that independence fundamentally means owning your rights and that sustainable careers require hard work rather than shortcuts. She stressed that quality matters more than quantity in a saturated streaming landscape where most tracks fail to gain traction.
